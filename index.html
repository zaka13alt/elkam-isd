From the Superintendent

A message to our families

“Our job is simple to say and hard to do well: make sure every child in Elkam walks across that stage with a plan they’re proud of.”

This fall, we’re focused on three things: getting reading right in the early grades, expanding career and college pathways at the high school level, and keeping our campuses safe and welcoming. Thank you for partnering with us — your involvement is the reason our schools work.

Literacy first

Every student reading on grade level by the end of 3rd grade.

Pathways

Twelve career certifications and dual-credit options at every high school.

Safe campuses

Secure entries, trained staff, and counselors on every campus.
